Place. Line up. Face.

Three steps, the same way every time, at home, in a hotel room or at a friend's house.

  1. 1Place

    Use your location or pick from 187 cities. The map draws the line to the Kaaba from where you are.

  2. 2Line up

    Lay the phone flat, top edge against a wall. Turn the dial until that wall on the map runs along the grid.

  3. 3Face

    Pick it up, hold it flat and turn on the spot until it says Facing the Qibla.

Phone compasses drift indoors.

A compass reads the Earth's magnetic field, and inside a building that field is bent by steel, speakers and wiring. The needle can be off with nothing on screen to show it.

Walls stay put. Once the map matches one, the direction is geometry, and your turn is measured by the gyroscope, which magnets don't affect.
